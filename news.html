Live Show | 8/10

Wolf Alice at Finsbury Park

Really great second time seeing Wolf Alice, with a stacked support list which included (Keo :D, and The Last Dinner Party). Highlights really being some of their heavier stuff; Yuk Foo, Play the Greatest Hits and Formidable Cool. White Horses is also an incredibly underrated tune, I still think they're just slightly off being the upper tier festival headliner although I'm not quite sure why...
